# Biography


Henrik Ferrara (1997), multidisciplinary musician and author, works with exploratory sound palletes. His work often embeds improvisation, reflecting a constant search for diverse timbres and methods of manipulating analog or digital sound objects. As well as seeking to incorporate the production of noise with greater visual expressiveness, he also researches the relationships between the audience, bodies in interaction with space and their gestures. His music often dialogues with video, performance and dance.
He develops new expressive interfaces, such as Crescente, an electroacoustic feedback instrument capable of producing immersive soundscapes. He often collaborates with Francisca Dores, such as in the latest installation "Linhas de Bordadura" (2025) and the performance project “fhosfhorus”, exploring symbioses of visual and sound feedback, and designs the sound for her films. He composed the album “ATAVÍSTICO”, with a visceral and submerged language, produced music with Guillio in “Songs For Green Tea and Peppermint Pope”, exploring noise rock and ambient abstractions. He directed Coro Normal with Simão Collares, creating a space for vocal and sound experimentation, and founded the collective OBSOLETO, of which the album "fábric" and the shows “Plasticidade” and “Trabalhar Rápido é Trabalhar Pior” stand out.
He created and performed in the “Pleasure Drone” project (with Gil Mac), where he explores dance music sonorities, expanding his sound universe to clubbing and rave. 
He has a degree in Audiovisual Communication Technologies (ESMAD) and a Master's degree in Sound Design and Interactive Music (FEUP). He currently lectures at the Escola Superior de Educação of the Universidade Politécnica de Viana do Castelo (UNIPVC). He is taking a PhD programme in Music - Artistic Practices in the Portuguese Context (DocMus) at ESMAE - Faculdade de Artes Performativas.
